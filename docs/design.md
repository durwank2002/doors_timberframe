# Design System Specification (`design.md`) - TimberCraft

## 1. Aesthetic Direction: Architectural Warmth & Artisan Timber
TimberCraft blends the tactile authenticity of seasoned hardwoods with high-precision architectural minimalism. The user experience evokes the atmosphere of a bespoke European or Scandinavian woodworking atelier.

## 2. Design Tokens
### 2.1 Color Palette
* **Woodland Dark (Backgrounds & Typography)**:
  - Deep Walnut / Noir: `#1c1917` (Stone 900)
  - Espresso Carbon: `#292524` (Stone 800)
  - Muted Charcoal: `#57534e` (Stone 600)
* **Warm Timber & Gold Accents**:
  - Raw Teak: `#d97706` (Amber 600)
  - Golden Oak / Honey Amber: `#b45309` (Amber 700)
  - Antique Brass: `#eab308` (Yellow 500)
* **Neutral Canvas**:
  - Sandstone Surface: `#fafaf9` (Stone 50)
  - Warm White: `#ffffff`
  - Subtle Border: `#e7e5e4` (Stone 200)

### 2.2 Typography
* **Headings**: Serif Elegance (`Playfair Display`, `Georgia`, or system serif) for title gravitas and artisan heritage.
* **Body & Labels**: Precision Sans (`Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`) for clear dimension metrics, price tags, and form fields.

## 3. Component Design Patterns
* **Card Surface**: Smooth rounded corners (`rounded-xl`), subtle stone borders, soft shadows (`shadow-sm` transitioning to `shadow-md` on hover).
* **Magnifier Zoom**: Interactive hover portal revealing ultra-crisp wood grain and carved reliefs.
* **Room Visualizer**: Dual-layer preview illustrating how a chosen door fits in living room, villa entry, pooja mandir, or modern minimalist environments.
* **Responsive Bottom Drawer**: Seamless touch-friendly bottom sheet for mobile filters and cart inspection.
